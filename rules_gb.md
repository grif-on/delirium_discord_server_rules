# Welcome!
## We hope you enjoy your time on this server, but first, please read the rules!

# Basic Server Rules
**1.1** Our server follows the [Discord Community Guidelines](https://discord.com/guidelines) and [Discord Terms of Service](https://discord.com/terms). This includes, among other things, ban evasion, threats of harm, sharing personal information, etc.
These form the foundation of our server; all other rules are additions to them. **If you cannot find a particular rule here, please refer to Discord's basic rules. Not knowing them does not exempt you from responsibility.**
**1.2** Deliberately harming or disrupting the server is prohibited. This includes organising or carrying out raids, mass spam, and other actions intended to disrupt the operation of the server or interfere with its members.
If there is reason to suspect that such actions are being carried out from an account as a result of it being hacked or otherwise compromised, moderators and administrators may temporarily restrict that account's capabilities while the circumstances are investigated.
**1.3** Any attempt to circumvent the server rules by any means is prohibited.
**1.4** You may not circumvent an imposed communication restriction by using another account or by any other means. An attempt to evade a restriction may result in the alternative account also being restricted and may affect your ability to appeal the original punishment. For more information about the punishment system, see **“Punishments for Rule Violations”.**
**1.5** Blank, inappropriate, offensive or obscene nicknames, profile banners, custom statuses and other parts of your profile are prohibited. Moderators and administrators reserve the right to require you to change individual parts of your profile if they do not comply with this rule. If this concerns your nickname, moderators and administrators may forcibly change it to a more appropriate alternative. If you refuse to cooperate with the server team, you will be removed from the server.
**1.6** Events and conflicts taking place outside our server are generally not subject to moderation. This includes personal disputes between users, private-message conversations and situations involving other Discord servers.
If such a situation directly affects our server or violates its rules, moderators and administrators may take any necessary action within their authority.
**1.7** In situations not explicitly covered by these rules, moderators and administrators may take any measures necessary to maintain order and server safety. Any measure taken will be proportionate to the nature of the situation and will not be used to punish a user arbitrarily.

# Language and Standards of Conduct
**Mandatory Rules**
**2.1** Personal attacks, harassment, defamation, deliberate provocation, trolling, sexism, racism and hate speech are prohibited.
*This includes conduct intended to insult, humiliate, intimidate, harass, pursue or deliberately provoke another user or group of people.*
**2.2** While on the server, you must communicate respectfully regardless of the other person's race, sex, language, religious or political views - even when they differ from your own.
**2.3** Political discussion is permitted on the server provided that all other server rules are followed.
**2.4** Using the server for organised political campaigning, mass messaging or persistent promotion of political material without permission from the administration is prohibited.
**2.5** Moderators and administrators reserve the right to remove threads or individual messages when rules have been violated, as well as when necessary to maintain order or prevent confusion.

**Recommendations**
**Language channels.** You may communicate in any language in any channel; however, we recommend following the language theme of each channel. This helps maintain order and makes communication more convenient for other users.
**Separate threads.** If a conversation becomes personal or remains focused on a single topic for an extended period, we recommend continuing it in a separate thread.
**Political discussions.** When discussing political topics, we recommend creating separate threads so that they do not become mixed with other conversations or make communication more difficult for other users.

# Content Posting Rules
*For the purposes of these rules,* **publishing and distributing content** *means any method of posting or displaying material on the server, including messages, files, images, video, audio, links and their previews, reactions, user profile content, and screen or video streams in voice channels.*

**3.1** Publishing or distributing material containing photorealistic depictions of violence, gore or animal abuse is prohibited, particularly when intended to harass or shock other people.
**3.2** As this server is centred around projects by the **B52 Development Community** and **B52 Development Team**, such content may only be shown in the context of those projects and where such material is or may be present within them.
**3.3** Content that may qualify as **NSFW (Not Safe For Work)** and is not prohibited by the rules above may be posted under the following conditions:
- Such NSFW content must either be hidden behind a spoiler **or** posted in an appropriate channel.
- If NSFW content is hidden behind a spoiler, you must include a content warning so that users can decide whether they wish to open it.
- If the content has been posted in an appropriate channel but needs to be mentioned in another, unsuitable channel, provide a link to the original message instead.

**3.4** Pornographic or erotic NSFW content may only be posted in channels marked 🔞. You can gain access to channels marked 🔞 by selecting the <@&1553744044549738527> role in the **“Channels & Roles”** tab.
**3.5** Follow the intended topics of channels when posting content. Channel topics are described in the Server Guide.
**3.6** Moderators and administrators reserve the right to remove messages that qualify as **NSFW**, may provoke controversy or may otherwise be inappropriate, even where the content does not constitute a rule violation.

Definition of **NSFW** for the purposes of the content-posting rules:
**NSFW (Not Safe For Work)** *is a label for online content containing material unsuitable for viewing in public or workplace environments, such as pornography, violence or shocking content. The label warns users that the material is best not opened in an unsuitable setting.*
*If, after asking yourself whether your content would be appropriate to view in a public or workplace environment, your answer is “no”, the restrictions above apply to that content.*

**4.1** Do not take moderation into your own hands or ask to be given a moderator role.

# Voice Channel Rules
**5.1** Playing irritating, excessively loud or piercing sounds through a microphone, Soundboard, TTS, bots or other Discord features is prohibited.
**5.2** When using the Soundboard, make sure that the volume is reasonable and does not exceed the average volume of normal conversation.
**5.3** Deliberately disrupting communication in voice channels is prohibited. This includes deliberately and repeatedly interrupting other participants or using Discord features to interfere with communication.
**5.4** Moderators and administrators may disconnect, mute or deafen a member, as well as move them into or out of a voice channel.
**5.5** Never stream anything that may contain personal or personally identifiable information, including, but not limited to, names, email addresses, telephone numbers, precise locations, personal/private messages, etc.

# Punishments for Rule Violations
Our server uses a progressive punishment system consisting of several levels. The specific measure applied depends on the nature of the violation, its severity and the surrounding circumstances.
Official warnings remain active for 2 weeks from the moment they are issued. Once this period expires, the warning is considered expired unless otherwise specified by the rules.
0. **Verbal Warning**:
For minor violations, moderators or administrators may issue only a verbal warning without applying an official punishment. A verbal warning does not count as an **official** warning within the punishment system.
1. **Timeout**:
A Timeout is the first **official** warning. The user's ability to interact with the server is temporarily restricted. A Timeout will normally last from several minutes to 24 hours, depending on the circumstances of the violation.
2. **Reprimand** (<@&1468583381104197703>):
A Reprimand is the second **official** warning. It is recorded on the user through a dedicated role and may also be accompanied by a temporary restriction on the user's ability to interact with the server (a Timeout).
3. **Communication Restriction** (<@&962822879173546116>):
If a user commits another violation after receiving two **official** warnings, a Communication Restriction may be imposed.
A **Communication Restriction** is an indefinite restriction on interaction with the server. The user retains access to the server and may view channels available to them and read messages, but loses the ability to interact with the server.
In particular, the user may not:
- send messages;
- reply to messages or use threads;
- create new threads or forum posts;
- add reactions;
- use commands or other features that allow interaction with the server;
- participate in voice channels.

A Communication Restriction remains in force indefinitely and may only be removed by the administration following consideration of an appeal. For more information about the requirements and appeal procedure, see **“Appealing a Communication Restriction”.**
4. **Ban**:
A Ban is the most severe measure and is applied in cases of serious rule violations, deliberate attempts to harm the server, raids and other situations in which the user's continued presence on the server is considered unacceptable.
A banned user completely loses access to the server.
**Exceptions**
The sequence described above does not apply in every case. Depending on the circumstances, moderators or administrators may:
- issue only a verbal warning;
- apply a more severe measure without prior warnings;
- skip individual stages of the punishment system;
- impose a Ban immediately for an exceptionally serious violation.

Some exceptions are specified separately in other sections of these rules.

# Appealing Moderation Decisions
Users have the right to ask a moderator to explain the reasons for a decision and the grounds on which a particular measure was applied.
If you have questions or disagree with a moderator's actions:
- You may contact the moderator who made the decision directly and ask which rules were violated;
- if you are not satisfied with the response or the matter cannot be resolved, you may contact the server administrators.
We aim to operate transparently and are willing to explain our actions in order to maintain a fair and comfortable environment on the server.

# Appealing a Communication Restriction
Appeals are considered individually and decisions are made by a majority vote of the administration.
An appeal may be considered provided that:
- more than 2 weeks have passed since the restriction was imposed or since the previous appeal was submitted;
- there have been no attempts to circumvent the restriction using an alternative account.

To appeal for the removal of a Communication Restriction, contact the restriction-appeals channel (<#1553682519608787064>) or contact one of the server administrators directly.
The appeal must include:
1. The date and time when the punishment was imposed;
2. The measures applied (for example: several consecutive official warnings or an immediate Communication Restriction);
3. The reason given by the moderators or administration;
4. Why the user believes the decision was incorrect or why the restriction should be reconsidered;
5. Any additional circumstances or evidence.

The information provided will be verified, and any discrepancies found may affect consideration of the appeal.
If a Communication Restriction is removed following an appeal, you will be given the **On Probation** role (<@&1553680806377947136>), which is equivalent to a **Reprimand** warning (<@&1468583381104197703>). The role remains active for exactly 2 weeks.
Any new violation committed during the **probation period** may therefore result in the immediate reimposition of the **Communication Restriction**.
If a Communication Restriction is imposed again, the ability to submit further appeals may be limited in light of previous decisions and the user's behaviour.
