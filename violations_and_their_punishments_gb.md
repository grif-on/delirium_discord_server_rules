**Basic Server Rules**

* **1.1** Violation of the Community Guidelines and Discord Terms of Service (threats, distribution of personal information, ban evasion) — **Serious / Severe / Extremely severe**.
* **1.2** Deliberate harm to the server — **Extremely severe**.
- If an account is suspected of having been compromised — **Severe**.
* **1.3** Attempts to circumvent the server rules by any means — **Severe**.
* **1.4** Circumventing an applied communication restriction using another account — **Severe** (results in the alternative account being banned).
* **1.5** Inappropriate profile (usernames, statuses, profile backgrounds) — **Minor** (however, refusal to change the profile when requested will result in removal from the server).
* **1.6** Events and conflicts occurring outside our server are generally not subject to moderation — *Not applicable (describes the authority of the administration)*.
* **1.7** Taking action in situations not covered by the rules — *Not applicable (describes the authority of the administration)*.

**Language and Conduct Rules**

* **2.1** Personal attacks, harassment, hate speech, provocation — **Serious**.
* **2.2** Disrespectful communication based on race, sex, religion or political views — **Serious**.
* **2.3** Discussion of political topics — *Not a violation (permitted provided that all other rules are followed)*.
* **2.4** Organised political campaigning and mass distribution without permission — **Serious**.
* **2.5** The right to remove threads/messages for the sake of maintaining order — *Not applicable (describes the authority of the administration)*.

**Content Publication Rules**

* **3.1** Distribution of photorealistic depictions of violence or animal cruelty — **Serious / Severe**.
* **3.2** Display of such content as part of B52 team projects — *Not a violation (exception to the rule)*.
* **3.3** Posting permitted NSFW content without a spoiler, warning or in an inappropriate channel — **Minor**.
* **3.4** Posting pornographic/erotic NSFW content outside 🔞 channels — **Minor**.
* **3.5** Posting content unrelated to the channel's topic — **Minor**.
* **3.6** Removal of controversial/NSFW content — *Not applicable (describes the authority of the administration)*.

**Text Chat Rules**

* **4.1** Unauthorised moderation and requests to be given a moderator role — **Minor**.
* **4.2** Mass posting of short messages within a short period of time, including through the use of @mentions — **Minor** (may escalate to a serious offence if carried out on a large scale).
* **4.3** Posting unapproved invitations to other Discord servers — **Serious**.

**Voice Channel Rules**

* **5.1** Annoying, loud or piercing sounds — **Minor**.
* **5.2** Exceeding the average volume when using the Soundboard — **Minor**.
* **5.3** Deliberately disrupting communication in voice channels — **Minor**.
* **5.4** The right to move, mute or disconnect members — *Not applicable (describes the authority of the administration)*.
* **5.5** Streaming content containing another person's private/personal information — **Serious**.
